THE DETAILS

Privacy, explained.

How information moves when you browse, enquire or arrange a service.

Content & sources reviewed: 17 Oct 2026 · Our source approach

Enquiry details

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email application or copies text to your clipboard. Its fields are not submitted to a website form server. We receive the information when you send an email, and use it to respond to your enquiry and arrange any agreed service.

Payments

When a Stripe payment link is available, checkout takes place on Stripe’s hosted website. Card information is entered there and is not collected by this website. Refer to Stripe’s privacy policy for its processing of payment data.

Website infrastructure

The site is hosted through Cloudflare. Hosting and security infrastructure may process request information, including IP address and browser details. The website does not store enquiry fields in browser storage. Campaign labels on payment links identify the website and selected service, and do not contain your enquiry details.

Your requests

To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have emailed us, contact info@cloudwindai.com. Retention may depend on an ongoing service or applicable record-keeping requirements.
