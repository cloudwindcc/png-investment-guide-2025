What this model assumes

The full investment is made at the start. Net cash flow is constant and received at the end of each year. The model includes no terminal sale value, further capital calls, financing structure or currency conversion. Taxes and operating costs must already be reflected in the net cash flow you enter.

Formula: NPV = −initial capital + Σ annual net cash flow / (1 + discount rate)^year. This is an illustrative calculation based on your assumptions.
