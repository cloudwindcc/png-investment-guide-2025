UNDERSTAND THE REGION

One ocean.
Distinct markets.

The South Pacific brings together established economies and island markets, connected by people, trade and the ocean. Each has its own business conditions, institutions and local priorities.

This field guide helps you explore that diversity, follow the primary sources and decide what to investigate next.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A useful place to begin.

Clear answers about the guide, its sources and the services available.

Where should I start when exploring South Pacific investment?

Define the business activity and shortlist markets before comparing specific projects. Our market guides link to the relevant investment agencies so you can check current requirements and discuss your proposed activity directly.

Which markets does this guide cover?

This guide currently covers Australia, New Zealand, Fiji, Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu, Samoa, Solomon Islands and Tonga. It is a selected regional guide rather than an exhaustive list of Pacific countries and territories.

Is Invest South Pacific a government agency?

Invest South Pacific is an independent information and consultation website. Links to government and investment agency resources are references; they do not imply endorsement, affiliation or a partnership.

How current is the information?

The review date identifies when the linked sources were checked for this edition. Requirements can change between reviews. Follow the official source for a market and confirm project-specific rules with the relevant authority before acting.

What services can I book?

Papua New Guinea services are US$199 for a one-hour telephone consultation, US$399 for local company registration, and US$999 for full-service agency support and local resource introductions. Other markets are quoted individually. Confirm scope, timing and any official fees before payment.

Does the scenario planner predict investment returns?

The planner calculates cash-flow yield, simple payback and net present value using inputs you provide. It uses no country or industry return assumptions and does not include a terminal sale value or currency conversion.
