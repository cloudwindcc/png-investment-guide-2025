Before you book.

What services can I book?

Papua New Guinea services are US$199 for a one-hour telephone consultation, US$399 for local company registration, and US$999 for full-service agency support and local resource introductions. Other markets are quoted individually. Confirm scope, timing and any official fees before payment.

When will the service take place?

We agree the consultation time or project schedule before payment. Registration and on-the-ground services depend on the jurisdiction, documents, counterparties and the agreed scope.

How are payment and cancellation handled?

Confirm the scope, total price and cancellation conditions before purchasing. Payment links, when provided, open Stripe’s hosted checkout. Contact us by email for a scheduling change, cancellation or refund request; the agreed service terms and applicable law govern the request.
