Questions to take forward.

  1. Confirm the activity

    Is the activity on a reserved or restricted list, and which registration documents are required?

  2. Test the operating assumptions

    Which site, utility connections, staff, suppliers and local relationships would the business need? Obtain project-specific evidence for each assumption.

  3. Build a realistic cash-flow case

    Separate start-up capital, recurring costs and expected cash receipts. Test more than one scenario and identify what would change the decision.
