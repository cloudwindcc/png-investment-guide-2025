Use of this guide

The website provides general regional and business information to support initial research. Official requirements can change. Verify project-specific legal, tax and investment matters with the relevant authority and appropriately qualified advisers.

Services and pricing

Listed fees apply to Papua New Guinea; other markets are quoted individually. Fees are in US dollars: US$199 for a one-hour telephone consultation, US$399 for local company registration, and US$999 for full-service agency support and local resource introductions. Before payment, confirm market availability, exact scope, timing, deliverables and any official or third-party fees. The total and any applicable taxes are confirmed before purchasing.

Agree the delivery schedule and cancellation conditions before purchase. For changes, cancellations or refund requests, contact us by email with your booking reference. The scope and conditions agreed for the service, together with applicable law, govern the request.

Official resources and outcomes

Linked agencies operate independently of this website. Registrations, approvals, counterparties and business outcomes are subject to their own requirements and decisions. Confirm the status of any proposed transaction before making a commitment.
